## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-trust, dependable, and forward-looking aesthetic tailored for enterprise IT hardware supply, infrastructure maintenance, and fleet repairs. The visual language bridges architectural calm and technical dynamism: warm architectural off-white surfaces prevent cold corporate fatigue, while electric and azure blues inject precision, velocity, and modern IT credibility.

The audience consists of IT Directors, CTOs, procurement leads, and enterprise operations managers who demand uptime, rigorous accountability, and seamless logistics. The emotional response is immediate confidence, clarity, and frictionless capability. 

The aesthetic is Modern Corporate Minimalism elevated by subtle tactile precision. Content is framed with crisp hairline borders, generous architectural whitespace, disciplined metric callouts, and clean geometric typography.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural off-white foundation with high-visibility electric blue accents derived from the brand identity.

- **Primary (`#0088EE`) & Electric Azure (`#00A3FF`)**: Applied to high-priority interactive touchpoints, key active indicators, progress metrics, and focal branded accents.
- **Secondary (`#0055AA`) & Deep Cobalt (`#0066CC`)**: Provide accessible contrast for text links, hovered button states, and deep enterprise accents.
- **Charcoal Deep (`#14181F`) & Charcoal Muted (`#525B6A`)**: Form a high-readability hierarchy for headlines, body copy, and secondary specifications without harsh black dissonance.
- **Surfaces**: Layered across `#FBFBF9` (warm structural canvas), `#F5F6F8` (subtle stone paneling), and `#FFFFFF` (pure white interactive cards and modal panels).
- **Hairline Borders (`#E3E7ED`)**: Provide structure across cards, tables, and compartmentalized modules.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, section overlines, index metrics, and UI action labels with **Inter** for dense transactional copy and multi-line body text. 

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked, crisp, and authoritative. Headings balance substantial weight (`700` and `800`) with clean geometry to convey strength and stability.
- **Eyebrows / Overlines**: Transformed to uppercase with `0.08em` tracking in electric azure or deep charcoal muted, acting as orientation anchors above every section header.
- **Step Counters & Indexes**: Numbered series (`01`, `02`, `03`) use bold tabular sizing to reflect procedural clarity and structured workflows.
- **Body & Data**: Inter provides uncompromised legibility across enterprise SLAs, service details, and technical spec tables.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts use a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum desktop container width of 1280px.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Component blocks favor structured split-views (e.g., 5-column brief / 7-column interactive card arrays).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Two-column service grids collapse gracefully.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` canvas margins. Horizontal scroll strips or single-column stacks handle multi-step sequences.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section groupings maintain `space-xl` internally, while distinct page segments are separated by generous breathing room (`4rem` to `6rem`) to honor the architectural aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive, murky shadows in favor of tonal surface nesting, refined hairline borders, and subtle luminous blurs.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#FBFBF9` warm off-white canvas with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules & Cards)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#E3E7ED`) and a micro-diffused ambient lift: `0px 2px 8px rgba(20, 24, 31, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Cards)**: Scaled border emphasis using primary light blue (`#0088EE` at 30% alpha) and an ambient shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 136, 238, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px rgba(20, 24, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Modals)**: Frosted backdrop surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, an off-white fill at 85% opacity, hairline bottom boundary, and `0px 12px 32px rgba(20, 24, 31, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, geometric shape language (`roundedness: 2`). Standard actionable surfaces, input fields, and metric containers utilize an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, projecting precision and technical sophistication without sharp harshness.

Larger layout containers, featured hero cards, and modal dialogs extend to 16px (`1rem`). Compact badges and interactive chips leverage a continuous pill radius (`9999px`) to create clear shape contrast against structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0088EE` electric azure background, `#FFFFFF` bold label, `0.5rem` border radius, and internal padding of `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, transitions to `#0066CC` with subtle `0px 4px 14px rgba(0, 136, 238, 0.3)` glow.
- **Secondary / WhatsApp Direct**: Surface-pure `#FFFFFF` with 1px border (`#E3E7ED`), `#14181F` text, and paired trailing icon (`→`). On hover, border shifts to `#0088EE` with soft azure fill wash (`#00A3FF` at 4%).
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless with `#0066CC` label and underline on hover.

### Cards & Service Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F6F8` with a 1px border (`#E3E7ED`) and `0.5rem` to `1rem` radius.
- Cards incorporate top-anchored numeric tags (`01`, `02`) in electric azure, clear section hierarchy, and clean divider hairlines between metadata fields (e.g., service scope, turnaround SLAs, hardware tiers).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped status badges (`0.25rem 0.75rem`) using subtle pastel-tinted backgrounds: active maintenance in soft azure (`rgba(0, 136, 238, 0.08)`) with `#0066CC` text; certified enterprise hardware in light neutral stone with deep charcoal text.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border in `#E3E7ED`, `0.5rem` corner radius, `0.75rem 1rem` padding, and `#14181F` text.
- Focus state: border shifts crisply to `#0088EE` with a matching `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.15)` focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square with 4px radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), `#FFFFFF` base with 1.5px border (`#525B6A`).
- Checked state: `#0088EE` fill with pure white icon glyph.

### Metric Callouts & Timelines
- Index numbers (`01` through `05`) presented in bold tabular figures paired with architectural divider lines (`1px solid #E3E7ED`), providing clear multi-step onboarding and service lifecycle navigation.
